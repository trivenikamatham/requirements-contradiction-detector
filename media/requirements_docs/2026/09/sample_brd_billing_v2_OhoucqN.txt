# BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)
# Project: MedSecure Billing, Outpatient Portal & Cloud Expansion
# Version: 2.0 (Modernization Initiative)
# Author: Hospital Enterprise Architecture Board

================================================================================
SECTION 1: CLINICIAN WORKFLOW & ACCESS CONVENIENCE
================================================================================

BRD-SEC-101: To prevent workflow disruptions during physician rounds, clinician and nurse sessions shall automatically expire after 30 minutes of idle inactivity.

BRD-SEC-102: Medical administrative staff may access patient billing histories using single-factor password authentication without requiring multi-factor authentication (MFA).

BRD-SEC-103: Clinician passwords must contain at least 8 characters.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: SEARCH SERVICE LEVELS & RESPONSE TIME
================================================================================

BRD-SLA-201: The unified patient search engine across regional partner clinics shall complete and render records within 3.5 seconds under standard hospital operating conditions.

BRD-SLA-202: Report generation for bulk outpatient billing statements shall conclude within 15 seconds.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: AUDIT TRAIL ARCHIVING POLICY
================================================================================

BRD-COMP-301: System audit logs and routine clinician access logs shall be stored in high-speed storage and retained for 90 days before automatic archiving and purging.

BRD-COMP-302: Audit records older than 90 days may be purged to manage primary storage costs.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: CLOUD TRANSFORMATION
================================================================================

BRD-INFRA-401: To eliminate on-site hardware maintenance overhead, all patient portal services and healthcare databases shall be deployed to AWS public cloud infrastructure.

BRD-INFRA-402: Clinical document attachments shall be stored as uncompressed JSON in a NoSQL MongoDB cluster.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: PATIENT ENGAGEMENT & OUTREACH
================================================================================

BRD-NOTIF-501: Automated notifications for upcoming appointments and pre-visit preparations shall be sent to patients via SMS text and email 24 hours in advance.

BRD-INTL-601: The patient portal must provide full multilingual internationalization support, allowing users to toggle between English, Spanish, and French.
